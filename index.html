Current R&D

MYCNC3D develops advanced technologies for next-generation additive manufacturing.

01 / MOTION

Multi-axis FDM

Development of multi-axis additive manufacturing architectures designed to overcome the limitations of conventional planar layer-by-layer printing.

02 / EXTRUSION

High-flow extrusion

Research and development of high-performance extrusion systems for significantly increased material flow rates and industrial-scale deposition.

03 / TOOLPATHS

Advanced toolpath generation

Development and evaluation of advanced toolpath strategies for non-planar and multi-axis FDM, including new slicing and motion-generation approaches.
